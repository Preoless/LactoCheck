What is lactose intolerance?

Lactose intolerance is a condition in which the body has difficulty digesting lactose, a natural sugar found in milk and dairy products. It can cause symptoms such as bloating, gas, stomach pain, and diarrhea.

LactoCheck is an educational tool and does not replace professional medical advice.

Food Alternatives

Some alternatives to common dairy products.

🥛 Regular Milk

Try lactose-free milk or unsweetened soy milk.

🧀 Regular Cheese

Consider products labeled lactose-free. Some aged cheeses naturally contain less lactose, but amounts vary.

🍦 Milk Ice Cream

Try a dairy-free frozen dessert made with coconut or another plant-based ingredient.

🥣 Cream

Try a suitable plant-based cooking cream, checking its label for milk ingredients.

🍫 Milk Chocolate

Look for dairy-free dark chocolate and always check the ingredients.

☕ Coffee Creamer

Try a plant-based creamer, but check for milk-derived additives.

Ingredient Guide

Learn about common ingredients found on food labels.

Lactose

Contains lactose

A naturally occurring sugar found in milk and dairy products.

Whey

May contain lactose

A milk-derived ingredient. Whey powder and whey concentrate can contain lactose.

Milk solids

Contains milk components

Components of milk that may include lactose.

Casein

Milk-derived

A milk protein. Casein itself is not lactose, but it indicates a milk-derived ingredient.

Lactic acid

Not automatically lactose

Despite its name, lactic acid is not the same as lactose.

Calcium lactate

Not automatically lactose

A calcium salt that is not the same thing as milk sugar.

Milk protein

Milk-derived

Indicates milk protein. Check the full label and allergen statement.

Non-dairy creamer

Check the label

Some products may still contain milk-derived ingredients such as caseinate.
