Dairy-Free Alternatives 🌱

Some options to explore when replacing dairy products.

🥛 Soy Milk

A plant-based alternative to cow's milk. Check labels for added dairy ingredients.

🌾 Oat Milk

A plant-based option for cereal, coffee, and cooking.

🥥 Coconut Milk

Commonly used in Filipino cooking. Check packaged products for added ingredients.

🌰 Almond Milk

A nut-based milk alternative. Avoid if you have an almond allergy.

🧀 Lactose-Free Dairy

Some dairy products have their lactose broken down. They still contain milk proteins and are not suitable for everyone with a milk allergy.

🍦 Sorbet

Some fruit-based sorbets are dairy-free. Always check the ingredient label.

Ingredient Guide 📖

Learn which ingredients deserve attention when checking food labels.

Lactose

Milk sugar. Its presence means the product contains lactose.

Milk Powder

Usually contains lactose. Check the specific product label.

Whey Powder

Often contains lactose. The amount varies by product.

Milk Solids

Milk-derived ingredients that may contain lactose.

Condensed Milk

Traditional condensed milk contains lactose.

Evaporated Milk

Ordinary evaporated milk contains lactose.

Casein / Caseinate

Milk proteins. They do not necessarily indicate lactose, but are important for people with milk allergy.

Milk Fat / Butter

Butter generally contains very little lactose, but dairy ingredients and amounts vary.

Lactic Acid

The name alone does not mean an ingredient contains lactose. Its source varies.
